⚙ UI Settings
Settings menu
General
How EVE Carbon integrates with your desktop.
Start with Windows
Launch EVE Carbon automatically when you sign in to Windows.
Minimize to tray
When minimized, hide EVE Carbon to the system tray instead of the taskbar. Click the tray icon to bring it back.
Jump Gate / Beacon Network Import
Paste a jump-gate list (e.g. a Webway export). Tab-separated columns, header optional: Region · System/POS · System/POS · Status · Owner · Password · Dist (ly) · Route · Friendly. Each row's two System/POS columns become an Ansiblex bridge used by the Jump Planner's Beacon mode.
Alliance Pack
Packs define SIG/Squad groups, icons, and comms channels for your alliance. Select a built-in pack or import one shared by your leadership.
Jabber Credentials
Stored locally and preserved until changed.
Ping Alert Sound
Plays when a director ping pops up. Choose a built-in sound or upload your own (.mp3 / .wav / .ogg). Drop files into assets/audio to add more built-ins.
Danger Zone
Permanently deletes all stored Jabber messages from the local database and clears saved credentials. This cannot be undone.
Forums
Embed your alliance forum in the Forums page. Enter the forum's base URL, then Log in once — your session is saved securely and the password is never stored.
Use the SAVE button below to save the URL.

GEZ Tracker
Paste the link to your GEZ tracker Google Sheet. EVE Carbon reads the “GEZ Rig List” tab (columns System · Structure · Name · Rig Slot 1–3) and turns each structure into a build location in the Blueprints calculator's My Structures dropdown — mapping the Upwell structure to its facility/size and reading the fitted manufacturing rigs for the ME/TE tier. The sheet only needs link-sharing (“Anyone with the link · Viewer”); nothing from it is stored beyond this session's structure list.
Forum Calendar (IP.Board)
Pull events from your alliance IP.Board forum calendar. The easiest way is the calendar's iCal/subscribe link (a personal feed link embeds your member key, so member-only calendars work without logging in). For member-only feeds without a key, use Log in to forum and tick “fetch via my login session”. Times appear in EVE (UTC) and local on the Calendar page.
Not logged in
Other iCal Feeds
Any other public iCalendar feed URLs (e.g. a Google Calendar basic.ics link), one per line.
You can also import a one-off .ics file directly from the Calendar page. Click SAVE to store feeds.

Syncing the station database downloads and resolves every NPC station and Upwell structure known to adam4eve — including full solar system, constellation, and region lookups via ESI.
This process takes 5+ minutes and runs in the background. You can continue using the app, but network activity will be elevated until it completes.
Updating the SDE downloads a ~130 MB file and requires an app restart to take effect.

ASSET DATABASE
Deletes every stored asset for all characters from the local database. Use this to clear stale or broken remnants — items that were moved or no longer exist but are still stuck in the list. Your assets reload automatically from ESI on the next background sync. This does not touch resolved station or structure names.
Background Wallpaper
Show an image behind the interface. Pick one below, or use Add image… to choose your own from disk. Images also load automatically from the app's assets/backgrounds folder.
Glass
Turns the interface into frosted glass. On Windows 11 the desktop behind the app shows through, blurred by the system acrylic — no wallpaper needed. One tint colours everything — the glass surfaces and the ambient glows — and by default it follows your operating system's accent colour. Your OS doesn't support acrylic — the glass look still applies, over the app's own background.
